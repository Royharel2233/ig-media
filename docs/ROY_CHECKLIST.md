# Roy's standing checklist - verify before EVERY reel delivery
(source: Roy's feedback through reel 1 v6, Oct 22 2026)

1. Voice: Gemini TTS, voice "Charon", brisk style prompt (clear, confident, authoritative Hebrew, brisk energetic pace, every word articulated), natural rate, NO digital speedup. gTTS/Avri/Hila/all Chirp 3 HD he-IL voices are REJECTED by Roy's ear.
2. Captions: Hebrew, Secular One, BIG (68px at 1080x1920, heavier outline ~3.2), progressive amber word-highlight (&H00B3FF&) tracking the voice, RLM for RTL line ends, readable at phone size.
3. Moving parts must visibly move: any wheel/gear in the story must visibly rotate in sync with the mechanism (teeth + bright marker dot so rotation is legible).
4. Physical correctness: escalator steps stay LEVEL on the passenger strand (incline + turnarounds) - a real staircase, not a tilted belt. Same standard for future machines: motion must match how the real thing moves.
5. Brightness: bright enough that machinery detail reads on a phone (exposure ~1.35 in the cine scene), while keeping the approved dark-studio cinematic look.
6. Frame fill: subject fills the frame - no large dead areas of empty floor/background.
7. QA gate: nothing goes to Roy until dense frame-by-frame QA on the ENCODED mp4 (2fps contact sheets, every frame inspected + full-res spot checks of key beats), with an honest defect list reported to parent first.
8. Music every reel: "Wholesome" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 3.0, at ~0.10 volume with fades; the credit line accompanies every delivery.
9. Fixed CTA on every reel: "מכיר מישהו שתמיד שואל 'איך זה עובד'? תשלח לו את זה."
10. Each reel has its own hook (escalator=mall visit, elevator=cables snap, microwave=center bowl mistake, induction=boils without heating, AC=doesn't make cold, removes heat).
11. One reel at a time: deliver, then STOP and wait for Roy's verdict before starting the next.
12. Every delivery includes: the mp4 (under WhatsApp size limit), a one-line Hebrew script summary, the music credit line, and the QA report (what was and wasn't verified, labeled ASR-proxy only for pronunciation - Roy's ear is the authority).
13. Be honest about what looks worse than the reference style; never claim parity we didn't verify.
14. Money: never spend Roy's money. Free tiers and his explicitly-enabled APIs only; anything costing money is his decision first.
