# Audiobooks as a business — qualified commercial verdict
Date: 2026-10-24. Prepared for Roy Harel's side-business evaluation. v3 (evidence-tiered, post-audit).

## Constraint note (corrected in v3)

Roy's constraint is that **he personally does no sales outreach/marketing activity**. Agent-run organic promotion (e.g., a Pinterest channel run on his behalf — a channel he already plans) is **not** ruled out by the constraints. It is, however, untested for audiobooks: no evidence was found that agent-run organic promotion produces audiobook sales for an unknown author, so it is treated below as an open possibility, not as a solution.

## The verdict

**No presently validated low-touch opportunity: as of October 2026 the research does not validate starting an audiobook business under Roy's constraints (≤1,000 ILS loss, ≤10 min/day, 90%+ automation, no sales activity by Roy himself).** This is a qualified commercial judgment, not a proven impossibility. What remains genuinely untested: (a) whether agent-run organic promotion can create an audience cheaply enough; (b) whether a non-US account is admitted to the KDP Virtual Voice beta; (c) actual payout mechanics to an Israeli bank at low revenue. The judgment rests on three legs:

1. **Roy has nothing to narrate.** His catalog is visual puzzle books; KDP's own suitability criteria exclude image/table-heavy books from audio, and no audio route turns a puzzle book into an audiobook. An audiobook business for Roy means first becoming an English-language *text* publisher. Note: no existing text is startup work, not proof that the ongoing 10-min/day constraint fails. Whether the continuing demands (human QA of agent-written text, a full listen-through of each production, reader acquisition) fit 10 min/day is **unresolved by the available evidence** — the research did not establish it either way.
2. **The biggest sales channels are closed to this exact model from Israel.** Audible is closed twice (ACX direct is human-narration-only, and ACX accounts are US/UK/CA/IE-only — both quoted verbatim below). Google Play Books does not accept sellers from Israel at all (official country table).
3. **Demand for AI-narrated titles from unknown authors is unproven at best; direct field evidence is weak-to-negative.** Industry-wide, AI-narrated titles were 0.03% of US audiobook revenue in 2025 (APA) — a market-share figure and a weak signal only, not a failure probability for a single quality title. Direct sample collected today (2026-10-24) on audible.com: over 50,000 results for narrator "Virtual Voice"; of the top 20 sorted by "Popular", 14 have zero ratings, and the six rated ones have 1, 1, 2, 2, 5 and 647 ratings (the 647 outlier is 28 months old). Ratings are not sales — most buyers never rate — so no sales inference is drawn; but a popularity-sorted catalog top with almost no ratings is a directional weak-demand signal. No source found shows a no-audience AI-narrated title earning meaningful income.

What would change the verdict: an existing ebook that already sells in text; agent-run organic promotion proving it can build an audience (untested for audiobooks); Audible opening to third-party TTS (officially "working on it", no date); KDP Virtual Voice admitting the account; or multi-voice AI quality at near-zero cost (currently the quality tier costs real money — Spoken, below).

## Evidence tiers (what is solid vs. directional)

### Tier A — official platform documentation (accessed 2026-10-24)
- **ACX (Audible direct), AI narration:** "Your submitted audiobook must be narrated by a human unless otherwise authorized: Unauthorized use of text-to-speech, AI, or automated recordings in ACX titles is prohibited... Audible is working to accept third-party TTS content from publishers and creators... and will share more updates on timing and availability as our offering evolves." — help.acx.com/s/article/what-are-the-acx-audio-submission-requirements (read directly on the official page via browser, 2026-10-24; the page is JS-gated to plain fetch).
- **ACX country eligibility:** "ACX is currently open to residents of the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Ireland who have a mailing address, valid local Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), and banking details for one of these countries." — help.acx.com/s/article/what-is-acx (read directly on the official page via browser, 2026-10-24).
- **KDP Virtual Voice:** invite-only beta; requires a Live KDP **eBook** with worldwide or US distribution rights, reflowable format, NCX TOC, primary language English/Spanish/Italian/French, **not public domain**, not image-heavy ("cookbooks or comic books would generally not provide the best listener experience"). 40% royalty on à la carte and membership sales; $3.99–14.99 price band; beta distribution is US-marketplace only at this time. Program Terms (section 2): "you retain all ownership rights in your Book" while "we retain ownership in the AVV Edition file" — i.e., the author keeps the book, Amazon owns the virtual-voice audio file; section 3 sets the 40% royalty. KDP Select enrollment adds exclusivity of the audio edition and subscription-fund royalties. Royalties paid monthly, ~60 days after month end. — kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJSXT4GZLP4PL62B (eligibility), GWHSFAK69P2537YR (Program Terms §2–3, quoted), GHJW2N8GLTQLK9TY (US-only beta / pricing), all read directly 2026-10-24.
- **Google Play Books:** Israel does not appear in the official seller sign-up / payments country table — no direct seller route from Israel, and auto-narrated creation is limited to listed countries. Auto-narration itself is free during beta, needs an EPUB on sale as an ebook, requires owning audiobook rights, with price-parity rules. — support.google.com/books/partner/table/6052428, answer/10013009. Google Play also no longer accepts public-domain content except from select partners (answer/1067634).
- **Spotify for Authors legal terms:** à la carte sales pay **50% of the author-set list price**; Premium-subscription listening pays a **pro-rata share of a per-market revenue pool based on consumption** (no fixed per-hour rate is published). — spotify.com/legal/spotify-for-authors-opt-in-distribution-terms. Spotify accepts digital-voice-narrated audiobooks with mandatory disclosure; digital-voice titles are not shared to referral partners. — support.spotify.com/us/authors/article/digital-voice-narration/.
- **Voices by INaudio:** "All Voices by INaudio services are available to authors in all countries." Author keeps 80% of royalties received; retailers pay 40–50% of list (à la carte), 30–40% (subscription), 25–45% (revenue share); monthly USD payouts. AI narration accepted only as an unmodified LPF package from Google Play Books, ElevenLabs, or Spoken Press. — voicessupport.inaudio.com articles 3222272, 3223552, 3222720; DVN FAQ via audiopod.ai matrix. Corporate status (Tier B): Findaway Voices was wound down by Spotify in May 2025 and wide distribution returned to the original founders' company, now branded INaudio — selfpublishingadvice.org, 2025-05-06. INaudio is therefore not described here as Spotify-owned.
- **Kobo Writing Life:** accepts AI narration (narrator credited as "Synthesised voice"); pays via EFT in USD 45 days after month-end, CAD 50 threshold. — kobowritinglife.zendesk.com articles 10563450280475, 360059385891.
- **Apple Books:** free digital narration is Apple's own program (English ebooks, via Draft2Digital/PublishDrive/Ingram, selective, 1–2 months processing). Apple also accepts third-party AI-narrated uploads via PublishDrive. — authors.apple.com/support/4973.
- **ElevenLabs:** commercial license from Starter tier ($6/mo); Studio has a "Publish to Spotify" LPF export via INaudio. — elevenlabs.io/pricing, elevenlabs.io blog 2025-02-20.

### Tier B — industry surveys (reputable, but note commissioners)
- **APA/Publishers Weekly (2026-06-08):** US audiobook revenue $2.43B in 2025 (+9%); 750k active titles (+43% in one year — supply flood); **AI-narrated = 0.03% of 2025 sales revenue**; willingness to try AI-narrated titles fell 70%→61% (2025→2026); only 16% of listeners have ever listened to an AI-voiced title. General fiction is the largest genre (27%); fastest-growing: humor, general fiction, children's/YA.
- **Edison Research at SSRS blinded study (July 2026, n=1,005, commissioned by Spoken, an AI-audiobook vendor — treat as vendor-sponsored):** blinded listeners rated Spoken's **multi-voice** AI narration comparably to a single human narrator for character-driven fiction (favorability 61% vs 53%; purchase intent 46% vs 49%, statistically comparable even after AI was revealed); 61% mistook the AI for human. For exposition without multiple characters, human narration was still preferred. **Limits of this evidence:** the study did not test flat single-voice AI against multi-voice; it cannot prove single-voice fails, and it does not establish that all fiction "needs" multi-cast. It shows one vendor's multi-voice product can pass a blind test; the production tier that passes costs money (below).
- **Spotify global expansion (newsroom, 2026-10-07):** audiobooks expanding from 22 to 180+ markets by end of 2026, including Israel as a *listening* market. (Listener availability, not author eligibility.)

### Tier C — field/community evidence (directional, not measured truth)
- **Direct sample by this research (primary, 2026-10-24):** audible.com US, narrator filter "Virtual Voice", sorted by "Popular": "1–20 of over 50,000 results". Of the top 20: 14 show "Not rated yet"; the six rated titles show 5.0★/1 rating, 5.0★/1, 5.0★/2, 5.0★/2, 4.4★/5, and 4.4★/647 ("The Coldest Hoe Ever", released 2024-06). Prices $3.99–$14.99, runtimes 50 min–16.6 h. Screenshots saved (repo, screenshots/ folder). **No sales inference:** ratings are not sales and Audible's "Popular" sort is opaque; this shows only that listener engagement at the top of the VV catalog is very thin.
- **audiobook.co.uk (secondary sample — their sampling, not ours, checked by them 2026-09-11):** 10,000+ Virtual Voice results on Audible UK, sold at the same prices as human narration, no way to filter them out. The listings they show are back-catalog nonfiction and classics (Mark Twain's Hawaii 13.5h; God: The Most Unpleasant Character in All Fiction 12.9h; The Oxygen Revolution 11.75h).
- r/selfpublish (Jan 2026), one user's manual sample of 7 bestselling Kindle books with Virtual Voice audio editions: audio editions ranked ~70k–370k in Audiobooks while the ebooks ranked top-2k–50k; 5 of 6 reviews mentioning the AI voice were 1-star; authors in-thread report quick returns and pulling AI editions. **Small informal sample — not a success rate.**
- r/selfpublish earnings thread (Apr 2025): multiple indie authors report audiobooks earning ~$0–30/month without an existing reader base; one 50+-title author cites 6–24 month ROI per title; the repeated pattern is "audio monetizes an audience you already have."
- BookLaunch News (Jun 2026) compiles ALLi-forum reports of Spotify Premium per-completed-hour payouts of ~$0.011–0.019 for indies. **Forum hearsay, unverified**; consistent with the official pool model but not an official rate.
- Counterpoints exist: some authors with existing readership report free KDP Virtual Voice adding meaningful sales; an established author reports Virtual Voice titles earning above his. All such positive cases share one precondition — a book that already sells.

## Conditional economics, separated by phase (assumptions labeled)

- **Phase 0 — text creation (the real cost).** An original English 25–30k-word nonfiction ebook. Cash cost can be ~$0 (agent-written), but this is days of production and QA work, and KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text. This phase, not narration, is where the business actually lives or dies. *Assumption: Roy wants agent-generated content; quality bar is his call. Whether ongoing text QA fits 10 min/day is unresolved.*
- **Phase 1 — narration production (wordcount → runtime → plan fit).** Typical narration pace is ~9,000–9,300 words per finished hour, so a 25–30k-word book is **~2.7–3.3 finished hours**; an 80k-word novel is ~8.6–8.9 hours. Single-voice: ElevenLabs Creator ($22/mo) carries a ~2h monthly character/credit allowance — **it does not cover one 25–30k book**; a full book needs the Pro tier ($99/mo, ~10h) or purchased credit top-ups, plus retake headroom of 10–20% for mispronunciations found in QA. Multi-voice quality tier (Spoken): $30/5k words pay-per-project → $180 for 30k words, $480 for 80k; $15/5k with a subscription → $90/$240 plus subscription fees. Plus a full listen-through QA at 1× runtime. *Vendor prices, accessed 2026-10-24.*
- **Phase 2 — distribution.** Uploads are free; INaudio takes 20% of receipts; Kobo/Spotify-direct have their own splits (above). Review times: days (Spotify) to 1–2 months (Apple). Israeli payout mechanics unverified until an account exists (gap 1).
- **Phase 3 — acquisition (the constraint-breaker).** No ad budget within 1,000 ILS across a whole catalog and no sales activity by Roy → organic discovery, plus untested agent-run organic promotion. Tier C evidence: unknown titles without an audience sit at ~$0–30/month. There is no Tier A/B evidence quantifying acquisition either way; treat any revenue projection as unproven.
- **Break-even arithmetic (stated price assumption).** At a $4.99 list price with 32–40% net of list (INaudio 80% × retailer 40–50%), one sale nets **~$1.60–2.00**. Covering $22 takes ~11–14 sales; $99 takes ~50–62; $180 takes ~90–113. Against Tier C reports of $0–30/month for unknown titles, even the lowest break-even is not assured.
- **Honest expected outcome:** unknowable from available evidence. Observed community outcomes for no-audience titles cluster near zero.

## Open gaps — what remains genuinely untested (these cut both ways; they are not "all easily fixable")
1. **Israeli payout mechanics (explicit status): UNRESOLVED.** How INaudio pays an Israeli bank account in practice — method (wire/PayPal/Payoneer), fees, and minimum threshold — is undocumented publicly and requires creating an account to confirm (not authorized). At the low revenues the evidence suggests, fixed payout fees or a high threshold could consume most or all earnings. Kobo publishes its method (EFT USD, CAD 50 threshold); INaudio does not.
2. Spotify for Authors direct sign-up availability in Israel — page section unreadable; the INaudio route bypasses it.
3. KDP Virtual Voice beta admission odds/timeline for a non-US author account — a rejection closes the only free production route.
4. Spotify Premium pool per-hour rates — officially unpublished; only forum estimates exist.
