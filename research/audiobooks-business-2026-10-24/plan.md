# Audiobook business deep research (for Roy)

Question: can creating and selling audiobooks be a viable business under constraints: <=1000 ILS max loss, <=10 min/day, 90%+ automation, no sales/outreach, free/local first, no new accounts without grant.

Subquestions:
- Demand/market size and growth 2025-2026
- Distribution platforms accessible from Israel (ACX, Findaway/Spotify, Google Play, Kobo, Apple)
- AI narration rules per platform (current)
- Economics: production cost, royalties, realistic sales for unknown author no marketing
- Copyright/public domain/AI voice licensing
- Competition/saturation, bad-review evidence
